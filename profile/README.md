**MegaZord #7563** is a team competing in the **FIRST® Robotics Competition (FRC)**, representing **SESI SENAI SP** in Jundiaí, Brazil.

As a high school robotics team, we are dedicated to advancing engineering, programming, and automation through collaborative projects, innovative solutions, and challenging technological experiences. We strive to push the boundaries of robotics while inspiring students to develop technical skills, creativity, and teamwork.

Over the past few years, MegaZord #7563 has became a national reference in robot programming within Brazil’s FRC teams community. Beyond developing competitive robots, we actively contribute to the teams growth conducting full training sessions and workshops focused on Java programming, sharing our knowledge and experience with other teams across the country.

For more informations about MegaZord #7563 visit our website: https://megazord7563.com.br/
